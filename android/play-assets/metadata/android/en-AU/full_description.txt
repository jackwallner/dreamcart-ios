DreamCart is a shopping game for the thrill of the haul without the bill. Browse original fashion from ten made-up boutiques, fill your cart, check out with play coins, and keep everything you buy in your own closet.

Free coins every day
Claim a Daily Drop, keep your streak alive for a weekly bonus, and spin the prize wheel once a day for up to 12,000 coins.

Shop thousands of pieces
A dozen new pieces drop every day across tops, dresses, outerwear, shoes, bags, jewelry, sunnies and hats. Search by brand, colour or style, or let For You learn your taste: pass left, cart right.

A checkout that feels real
Pick a pretend delivery address or skip it, watch the order total, and place the order. Your haul lands straight in your closet. Nothing ships and nothing is charged to a card.

Build your dream closet
Everything you own stays in your closet, ready to search, sort and revisit.

Browse without an account
The whole catalog is open to browse right away. Sign in when you want coins, a cart and a closet that sync to your account.

Optional extras
Coin packs top up your wallet when you want to go big. DreamCart+ is an optional weekly membership that doubles your daily coins and lets you spin the wheel as often as you like. It renews automatically until you cancel in Google Play.

Coins are for use inside DreamCart only and have no cash value. All items and delivery are virtual.
