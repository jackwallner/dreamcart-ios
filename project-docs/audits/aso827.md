# DreamCart ASO Audit

Audit date: 2026-08-27  
App Store ID: `6787820106`  
Primary market: United States  
Current version: 1.0.1, Ready for Sale  
Implementation status: recommendation only, no metadata or screenshots changed

## Executive directive

Keep the `DreamCart` brand, current App Store title, current subtitle, and current hidden field as the next release control. The title owns the app's only stable US search cluster, #4 for `dopamine shopping` across 43 Astro observations. The subtitle and 100-byte keyword field already create high-value combinations such as outfit maker, outfit planner, outfit creator, fashion shopping, clothes game, retail therapy, wardrobe planner, and shopping simulator.

Do not respond to unranked broad terms by rewriting all indexed fields. The app has meaningful US page-to-download conversion in the two supplied download weeks, but a weak impressions-to-page-view rate. That pattern points first to search qualification and grid persuasion, especially localized overexposure and screenshot framing.

Replace the screenshot package with six canonical product-evidence frames. The current five frames violate the one-header contract, use unsupported emotional copy, obscure literal UI under the floating tab bar, and omit actual virtual checkout, outfit building, and For You recommendations.

After the screenshot baseline stabilizes, test one hidden-field variant that replaces generic `app` and low-value `glam` with literal `virtual`. Do not change the title or subtitle during that test.

## Do not implement

- Do not replace `Dopamine Shopping` with `Fashion Shopping` in the first release. The current phrase ranks #4 and `fashion shopping` is outside Astro's range.
- Do not imply real retail, physical delivery, discounts, cash value, resale value, or real-world ownership.
- Do not claim therapy, treatment, relief from addiction, guaranteed mood improvement, or freedom from buyer's remorse.
- Do not call the fictional shipping-address flow real fulfillment.
- Do not claim AI styling unless a current product surface and model prove it.
- Do not use `Spin to win` in screenshots. The phrase creates unnecessary gambling and pay-to-win ambiguity beside coin packs.
- Do not show prices, subscription terms, or coin-pack upsells in the primary screenshot carousel.
- Do not publish translated headers over English literal UI as localized screenshots.

## Positioning and claim boundaries

Recommended position:

> A virtual fashion shopping game where daily coins become carts, closets, and outfits

Supported product truth:

- Original virtual fashion catalog
- Daily coin drop
- Free wheel spin
- Virtual cart and coin checkout
- Unboxing moment
- Owned closet collection
- Outfit building
- For You recommendations
- Coin packs
- Optional DreamCart+ with current benefits defined by the binary and store configuration

Required qualifiers:

- Items are fictional and have no cash value.
- No physical products ship.
- The shipping-address screen stores a fictional local address for the role-play flow. Do not claim no address is ever entered.
- `Dopamine` is brand positioning, not a neurological or mental-health claim.
- `Retail therapy` may be a searched idiom, but public copy must not promise therapeutic benefit.
- Trial and subscription language must match current eligibility, renewal, cancellation, and regional pricing.

The current public website and localized descriptions under-explain DreamCart+ while the binary and StoreKit path actively sell it. Repair that truthfulness separately from the indexed-field experiment.

## Screenshot audit

### Live inventory

Versions 1.0 and 1.0.1 contain the same five `APP_IPHONE_67` assets. The live files are byte-identical to local fastlane and docs copies:

1. Shop
2. Coins
3. Cart labeled as Checkout
4. Closet

Final files are 1320 by 2868 RGB. Raw captures are 1206 by 2622 RGBA. Dimensions are accepted, but the set is staged proof rather than canonical current-build evidence.

### Current defects

- Every frame uses a two-line headline plus a three-line subtitle.
- Every headline's second line ends with a period.
- The compositor begins headers at 138 px, below the 140 px minimum.
- Copy is mechanism-led: `Claim coins every day`, `Checkout hits different`, and `Spin to win more coins`.
- The Cart frame is mislabeled as Checkout and does not prove the actual virtual-order disclosure.
- The floating tab bar covers product names, cards, or rarity badges in Shop, Coins, and Closet.
- The set omits actual CheckoutView, Outfit Builder, For You recommendations, and owned-look composition.
- `Spin to win` creates avoidable gambling ambiguity.
- `Shop without the guilt` and buyer's-remorse language are unsupported emotional claims.
- Capture uses fixed coordinates and sleeps without deterministic backend fixtures or readiness gates.
- Pollinations and Supabase changes can place missing art, shimmer, 429 errors, or a different catalog order into captures.
- There is no manifest, capture report, flow mapping, sidecar, mask, search grid, or canonical audit.
- Current screenshots predate live build 26.
